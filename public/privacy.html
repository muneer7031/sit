Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to answer your enquiries and do business with you. We don't sell your information, we don't use advertising trackers, and our website doesn't set cookies.

This policy explains how Zetarah Exports ("we", "us"), a seafood export business in Chandiroor, Cochin (Kochi), Kerala, India, handles information about people who visit our website, send us an enquiry, or message us on WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

  • Enquiry form. The details you type: your name, company, email address, country, the product you're interested in, volume, destination port and your message.
  • WhatsApp messages. When you message our WhatsApp Business number, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the content of your messages (text, and a note of any photos, documents or voice messages you send), along with delivery and read receipts.
  • Emails and calls. Whatever you choose to share with us when you write or call.
  • Website visits. Simple, anonymous statistics: which pages and products were viewed, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer), the website that referred you, and an approximate country worked out from your device's time-zone setting. We do not store your IP address for this and we don't use cookies.
  • Spam protection. To stop automated spam, the enquiry form briefly checks how quickly it was filled in, and we keep a one-way scrambled code ("hash") of your network address to limit repeated submissions. The original address can't be recovered from it.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or message and to prepare offers, quotations and contracts.
  • To arrange and follow up orders, shipments, documents and payments.
  • To keep business records, as required for trade and tax purposes.
  • To understand, in general terms, how our website is used, so we can improve it.

We don't use your information for automated decisions about you, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Services that process information for us

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our website and office systems. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): carries WhatsApp messages between you and us.
  • Supabase: securely stores our business records, enquiries and messages, with access limited to our team.
  • Vercel: hosts our website.
  • Resend: sends notification emails to our own team (for example, to tell us a new enquiry arrived).
  • Google (Gemini): we may use Google's AI service to help read a message and fill in an order form, which a person always checks. We currently use Google's free tier, under which Google may use the content sent to it to improve its products. We only do this for business messages about products, quantities and prices.

Some of these providers may store or process information outside India. We choose providers that protect information with appropriate security.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries, messages and business records for as long as we have a business relationship with you, and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting reasons. Anonymous website statistics are kept in summary form. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

5. Security

Our office system is private, protected by individual logins (with two-step login available), and each team member can only see what their role needs. Information travels over encrypted connections.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.
  • You can ask us to delete your information, unless we must keep certain records by law (for example, invoices).
  • You can stop messaging us on WhatsApp at any time, or block our number.

7. How to delete your data

Email sitharaexports@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", and include the email address or WhatsApp number you used with us. We'll confirm when it's done, normally within 30 days.

8. Children

Our website and services are for businesses. They are not intended for children, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
